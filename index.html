cs.CV 2026-10-24
SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning
Yuhan Liu et al.
Abstract
Training generalizable embodied agents requires realistic, varied, and simulation-ready indoor environments. Language-guided methods support semantic planning and object-level reasoning, but precise spatial grounding remains difficult. Visual generative models offer rich priors over plausible arrangements, yet their outputs require geometric reasoning to become physically valid 3D scenes. We introduce SceneBloom, an agentic framework that combines visual layout sampling with contract-guided 3D compilation. A hierarchical sampler uses diffusion priors to generate furniture arrangements within functional zones. A compiler then uses relations extracted from each proposal to guide geometric fitting and local pose correction. Across five room types, SceneBloom attains the highest visual quality and physical stability among the compared methods and is the only method with no detected mesh collisions and out-of-bounds objects. Its layouts are also closer to the distribution of real rooms than those of all compared baselines. Object navigation policies trained in these realistic scenes achieve a higher success rate in held-out real scans than those trained on an equal number of real training scans. Code is available at https://github.com/Tinaliu0123/scenebloom.

RobustRLlib: A Unified Library and Benchmark for Robust Reinforcement Learning Algorithms
Xi Chen et al.
Abstract
Robust reinforcement learning (RL) is critical for reliable real-world deployment, where policies may fail under diverse shifts and disruptions. Existing robust RL algorithms offer a rich set of mechanisms and principles, yet two major barriers limit their broader potential: limited algorithm reusability and an unclear empirical capability boundary. We introduce RobustRLlib, an algorithm-centric library and benchmark that addresses both challenges. It integrates 17 representative robust online, offline, and safe RL algorithms under a unified interface for substantially improved reusability, together with an enhanced evaluation platform spanning diverse real-world shifts and advanced humanoid and vision-language-action tasks. Systematic evaluation over 273 perturbation configurations provides an up-to-date capability map of current robust RL algorithms, revealing where they remain effective and where substantial robustness gaps persist. As an open-source, user-friendly platform, RobustRLlib offers a reproducible and extensible codebase for applying existing methods to new problems and developing new robust RL algorithms.

DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection
Amber Xie et al.
Abstract
Collecting high-quality demonstrations remains a central bottleneck for learning dexterous manipulation. Teleoperation records executable robot actions, but it requires a complete robot setup and places the burden of coordinating many finger joints on the operator. Human demonstrations are easier to collect, but their motions must be estimated and retargeted to the robot, and they do not directly provide robot actions. In this paper, we introduce DexPort, a portable data-collection interface for high-DoF robot hands. It uses the same hand hardware for collection and deployment, avoiding a hand embodiment gap, and records both commanded joint targets and measured joint states. Our key observation is that a small set of skills can support a wide range of dexterous tasks. During collection, the operator moves the hand-held interface to specify the robot wrist motion and selects or adjusts skills that coordinate the finger motion. This removes the need to control each joint independently while retaining behaviors such as precise grasping and in-hand manipulation. We collect demonstrations across several real-world tasks and in unstructured environments without a robot arm. We then use behavior cloning to train visuomotor policies directly on the recorded wrist motions and hand commands, without skill labels. We evaluate across 4 tasks: pick-and-place with diverse objects, screwing with in-hand tool use, drawer stowage which requires simultaneous grasp and pulling, and a bimanual scanning task. We collect over 2,500 demonstrations with our portable device, in both laboratory and in-the-wild settings. Across our tasks, we see an improvement from 11% to 64% when using recorded robot commands instead of states, and an improvement from 36% to 78% for task success when using in-the-wild data.

Flownomaly: Single-Step Conditional-Mean Recovery for Few-Shot Anomaly Detection
Bernardo Perrone Ribeiro et al.
Abstract
Few-shot industrial anomaly detection requires modeling category-specific normality from only a handful of defect-free images. Common approaches tie inference to stored exemplars, impose rigid linear structure, or risk reproducing anomalous content. Flownomaly instead casts the problem as normal-counterpart recovery in frozen DINOv2 feature space. Given normal supports, a velocity field is learned through conditional flow matching over frozen patch tokens to transport Gaussian noise toward normal features. Along straight paths, a single recovery step using one evaluation of the optimal field yields the posterior mean of the clean normal token without trajectory integration. The operating time governs how strongly recovery modifies a query token. With no anomalous validation samples available for tuning, a normal-only Jacobian gain-to-noise criterion selects this time from the supports alone. Squared residuals between query tokens and their recovered counterparts yield anomaly signals for both dense localization and image-level detection. Across 1-, 2-, and 4-shot benchmarks on MVTec-AD and VisA, together with sample-shot evaluations on Real-IAD, Flownomaly achieves state-of-the-art performance against recent category-specific methods. In the 1-shot setting, image- and pixel-level AUROC reach 97.8% and 98.0% on MVTec-AD and 95.3% and 98.7% on VisA, with a 2.1-point image-level AUROC improvement over the strongest baseline on VisA. Code is available at https://github.com/b-rbmp/Flownomaly.
cs.CL 2026-10-24
Measure Before You Train: Typed Decision Models vs. a Classical Baseline vs. a Generative LLM for Routing a Personal Multilingual Inbox
Federico Lucca
Abstract
Personal email agents decide, for every message, what it is, whether it is urgent, whether to draft a reply, create a task, or ignore it. Their error rate is rarely measured: the stored labels were written by the system itself. We present Foyer, a self-hosted personal inbox agent in daily use, and a measurement protocol (stratified sampling, frozen snapshots, thread-grouped splits, pre-registered hypotheses, human labels in the agent's review loop) that yields ground truth before any training. On 739 real Italian/English emails labelled by the owner (231 unbiased, 500 adversarially sampled), we compare nine systems under thread-grouped cross-validation: the production keyword rules, a TF-IDF + logistic-regression baseline, local typed decision models (Tev1, Nimble), a hosted decision model (Nimble v4-max), and hosted generative LLMs (Qwen2.5-7B, Gemma 4 26B/31B). Four results. The production rules score 28% on message type against a 58% majority class: the field shown most prominently carried no information. With ~200 labels, logistic regression has the highest accuracy on every field (type 0.79, urgency 0.74, needs-reply 0.83, is-noise 0.90), though it never predicts the two rarest classes and is statistically tied with Gemma 4 on type; few-shot prompting does not close the gap. The sender's relationship (client, supplier, service) is recovered from other emails of the same contact with 98.6% accuracy where the contact has been seen, and is not inferable from a single message by any model: it belongs in the contact graph, not the prompt. Finally, accuracy is not the product metric: composed into actions, the most accurate system most often ignores emails needing action (18% vs 4% for the best LLM); a cost-chosen threshold on its noise probability halves that rate and makes it the cheapest system.

Where Rounding Happens Matters: Rounding-Location Alignment Stabilizes LLM Reinforcement Learning
Yijing Ke et al.
Abstract
Reinforcement learning for large language models often uses separate systems for inference and training. Even with identical weights and BF16 tensors, implementation differences can make an intended on-policy update off-policy and destabilize training. Existing remedies align the entire inference and training stacks, change global precision, or modify the learning algorithm. We ask whether aligning selected parts of the execution stack can stabilize BF16 RL while other implementation differences remain. We align training-side rounding locations with inference in three regions---RoPE, SwiGLU, and residual RMSNorm---by retaining selected intermediates in FP32 until their BF16 outputs. Across four Qwen-family models, aligning all three regions prevents the collapse observed under the default BF16 baseline; RoPE-path alignment alone also suffices in the studied runs, despite remaining numerical discrepancies. The interventions keep the inference engine, matrix-multiplication and attention kernels, and learning objective unchanged.

Beyond Discrete Tokens: Preserving Numerical Continuity in Medical Vision-Language Models
Dianye Huang et al.
Abstract
Objective: To develop a unified vision-language model for prompt-conditioned quantitative ultrasound measurement that addresses the mismatch between continuous measurement targets and discrete token-based numerical prediction. Methods: This article presents HAD-VLM, a hybrid autoregressive-diffusion framework that combines autoregressive text generation with continuous measurement prediction within a shared backbone. Visual prompt tuning adapts the vision encoder to ultrasound images. A diffusion process predicts a five-component representation comprising caliper endpoint coordinates and measurement length. A post-processing strategy combining the proposed coordinate-length consistency mechanism with ensemble prediction is introduced to refine measurement estimates. We evaluate four echocardiographic measurement tasks on EchoNet-LVH, with additional assessments of medical question answering and X-ray vertebral measurement. Results: HAD-VLM achieves mean absolute errors of 1.36, 2.77, 3.05, and 1.16 mm for IVSd, LVIDd, LVIDs, and LVPWd, respectively, reducing errors by 21.4-37.6\% relative to the Vicuna-based autoregressive baseline. It also outperforms fine-tuned Qwen3-VL and direct MLP regression across all four tasks. Medical question answering performance is preserved on the evaluated dataset. For X-ray vertebral measurement, HAD-VLM reduces mean absolute error from 11.4 to 6.7 pixels. Conclusion: Integrating autoregressive language generation with diffusion-based continuous prediction improves quantitative medical image measurement while retaining prompt-conditioned task flexibility. Significance: HAD-VLM provides a unified approach to combining language interaction with anatomically grounded numerical prediction for quantitative medical image analysis.

Monetizing Intelligence: Advertising and Subscription Design on LLM Platforms
Yuhang Ji et al.
Abstract
Large language model (LLM) platforms introducing advertising must decide how much capability ad revenue should fund and what consumers should pay to avoid ads. We study a monopolistic platform that jointly chooses costly response quality, advertising intensity, and subscription prices when advertising reduces user value but better service raises advertising returns. Three findings follow. First, when partly ad-supported and ad-free plans coexist, advertising can finance useful service for consumers who receive none under pure subscription. Lower effective quality in middle plans weakens the alternatives available to premium users, allowing higher prices for unchanged ad-free service. Some fully ad-supported users gain utility, while partly ad-supported and ad-free users lose: broader access can coexist with utility polarization. Second, an intermediate advertising cap can maximize consumer surplus by preserving some revenue that finances service while limiting degradation. Third, cheaper compute can raise quality and reduce ad intensity, yet sufficiently large cost reductions can increase aggregate advertising revenue above its initial level because better service raises the value of the remaining advertised interactions. An extension shows how advertising-funded upgrades can bring plans to the same maximum capability and advertising exposure, eliminating the basis for different prices. These results make menu design central to evaluating LLM advertising: the consequences extend beyond users who see ads to the alternatives and prices faced by those who do not.
cs.IR 2026-10-24
Knowledge Distillation across Item and Code Spaces for Parallel Generative Recommendation
Yixuan Xu et al.
Abstract
Generative recommendation with semantic IDs represents each item as a sequence of discrete codes derived from its semantic representation and predicts these codes to generate recommendations. Existing semantic-ID recommenders are typically trained with hard labels for the observed next item. Although such supervision identifies the target, it provides no explicit distributional information about alternative items conditioned on the same user history. Sequential recommenders can supply this item-level predictive knowledge, but transferring it to semantic-ID generation is challenging because the teacher and student operate in item and code spaces, respectively. We propose two complementary knowledge-distillation objectives for parallel semantic-ID recommenders, which predict all code positions simultaneously without autoregressive prefix conditioning. Item Distribution Matching (IDM) reconstructs a catalogue-level item distribution from the student's parallel code predictions and aligns it with the teacher's item distribution. Code Marginal Matching (CMM) projects the teacher's item probabilities onto each code position using fixed item-to-code assignments, then matches the resulting code marginals to the corresponding student heads. Both objectives transfer the teacher's soft distributional knowledge while preserving the student's parallel prediction structure. Experiments on benchmark datasets show that both methods consistently outperform existing state-of-the-art approaches. On average, IDM improves Recall@10 by 15.39%, while CMM improves NDCG@10 by 8.28%. These results demonstrate that distillation in either reconstructed item space or code-marginal space can substantially strengthen hard-label supervision for parallel generative recommendation.

If at First You Don't Succeed, Backtrack: Lightweight Recovery for Frozen VLAs
Thomas Landeg et al.
Abstract
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models perform well on robotic manipulation, yet remain brittle once their own actions carry them into states their training data barely covers. Recovering from such failures usually requires recovery demonstrations, policy retraining, or a separate model at inference time. We introduce Backtrack, a lightweight failure detection and recovery mechanism for frozen VLAs that needs none of these. A small failure prediction head, trained only on outcome-labeled rollouts of the frozen policy, reads the VLA's existing internal representations to estimate whether the current episode will fail. When the predicted failure probability exceeds a threshold, a controller pauses the policy, retraces the arm's recently achieved motion, and hands control back to the unchanged policy so it can retry from an earlier configuration. The head adds only 0.48-0.88% to the policy's parameter count. We evaluate Backtrack on LIBERO with three VLAs spanning two action-generation families: SmolVLA and $π$0.5, which use flow-matching action experts, and the autoregressive $π$0-FAST. On LIBERO-Long, the suite of long-horizon multi-step tasks and the one on which all three policies fail most often, Backtrack reduces each policy's failure rate by 19-48%, raising success by up to 7.4 percentage points; on the three shorter suites, where control success is already 85-99%, its effect is smaller. On MetaWorld, all twelve controller configurations tested with SmolVLA improve on the unassisted policy.

  • 러브레이스의 반박 (Lovelace's Objection)
    기계는 우리가 명령한 것만 수행할 수 있으며, 스스로 생각하거나 진실을 예상할 수는 없다고 주장. 이는 훗날 앨런 튜링이 반박하고, 1960년대 IBM이 AI에 대한 대중의 공포를 잠재우는 논리로 사용하는 등 '기계 지능' 논쟁의 시발점이 됨.

1945: 폰 노이만 구조의 정립 (EDVAC)
  • EDVAC 보고서 초안 (First Draft of a Report on the EDVAC)
    폰 노이만이 기차 안에서 작성한 미완성 보고서. 에니악의 후속 모델인 EDVAC의 논리적 구조를 정리했으나, 골드스타인이 폰 노이만 단독 저자로 배포하면서 컴퓨터 설계의 표준 지침서가 됨.
  • 폰 노이만 구조 (Von Neumann Architecture)
    중앙처리장치(CPU), 저장장치(Memory), 입출력 장치(I/O)로 구성되며, 프로그램 내장 방식을 통해 하드웨어 변경 없이 소프트웨어만으로 다양한 연산을 수행하는 현대 컴퓨터의 표준 아키텍처.
1945: 기술 소유권과 오픈 소스 정신
  • 특허 vs 공공재 (Patent vs Public Domain)
    기술의 상업화를 위해 특허를 원했던 모클리·에커트와 달리, 폰 노이만은 지식을 인류 공공의 자산(Public Domain)으로 공개하려 함. 이로 인해 '아이디어 강탈' 논란과 갈등이 발생.
  • 현대적 의의
    폰 노이만의 개방적 태도는 현대 AI 연구계의 오픈 소스 문화와 맞닿아 있으며, 기술 독점보다는 집단 지성을 통한 혁신과 공유를 중시하는 철학적 배경이 됨.
1946: 현대 컴퓨터의 탄생
  • 에니악 (ENIAC)
    최초의 범용 전자식 컴퓨터 등장. 진공관을 사용하여 이전의 기계식 장치와는 비교할 수 없는 계산 속도를 제공, AI 알고리즘이 구동될 수 있는 물리적 기반 마련.
1947: 소프트웨어와 디버깅
  • 그레이스 호퍼 (Grace Hopper)
    마크 1의 프로그래밍을 주도하며 컴파일러(Compiler)의 개념을 최초로 개발. 훗날 COBOL 언어 개발의 주역이 됨.
  • 버그 (Bug)
    마크 2 가동 중 릴레이에 낀 나방을 발견하고 '버그'라고 기록한 것에서 유래. 컴퓨터 오동작의 원인을 찾는 '디버깅(Debugging)' 용어의 시초가 됨.
1948: AI의 전조
  • 노버트 위너(Norbert Wiener)
    동물과 기계의 통제 및 통신 이론인 Cybernetics 출간. 생물학적 뇌 구조를 기계적으로 모방하려는 시도로, 현대 AI 연구의 이론적 뿌리가 됨.
1951: 최초의 신경망 기계
  • SNARC
    마빈 민스키가 개발한 아날로그 신경망 계산기. 강화 학습을 통해 쥐가 미로를 찾는 행동을 모방.
1952: 자동화된 추론
  • Logic Theorist
    앨런 뉴웰과 허버트 사이먼이 개발. 인간의 문제 해결 능력을 모방하여 수학적 정리를 증명한 초기 AI 프로그램.
1950: AI의 태동
  • 앨런 튜링(Alan Turing)
    'Computing Machinery and Intelligence' 논문 발표. 기계의 지능을 판별하는 기준인 Turing Test 제안.
1956: 용어의 탄생
  • 다트머스 회의(Dartmouth Workshop)
    존 매카시(John McCarthy)가 'Artificial Intelligence'라는 용어를 최초로 사용.
1958: 신경망의 시초
  • 프랭크 로젠블라트(Frank Rosenblatt)
    초기 인공신경망인 Perceptron 고안. 학습을 통해 기계가 패턴을 인식할 수 있는 가능성 제시.
1959: 머신러닝의 태동
  • 아서 사무엘 (Arthur Samuel)
    체커 게임 프로그램을 통해 기계가 명시적 프로그래밍 없이 경험을 통해 학습할 수 있음을 증명하고 '머신 러닝' 용어 창시.
  • Pandemonium & Advice Taker
    올리버 셀프리지의 경쟁적 특징 인식 모델(Pandemonium)과 존 매카시의 상식 추론 시스템(Advice Taker) 제안.
1965: 전문가 시스템
  • DENDRAL
    화학적 분석을 수행하는 최초의 전문가 시스템(Expert System). 특정 분야에서 인간 전문가 수준의 수행 능력 입증.
1966: 최초의 챗봇 ELIZA
  • 요제프 바이트첸바움(Joseph Weizenbaum)
    MIT에서 개발한 심리 상담 프로그램. 단순한 패턴 매칭만으로도 인간이 기계와 정서적 교감을 나눌 수 있음을 보여줌(Eliza Effect).
1974: 첫 번째 AI 겨울
  • Minsky & Papert
    Perceptron이 XOR 문제를 해결하지 못한다는 한계 증명.
  • Lighthill Report
    미국/영국 정부의 연구 자금 대거 삭감으로 인한 침체기.
1980s: 퍼지 이론과 가전 혁명
  • 퍼지 제어(Fuzzy Control)
    딥러닝 이전, 세탁기, 밥솥 등 가전제품의 주류로 부상.
  • 특징
    인간의 애매모호한 언어 표현을 수학적으로 처리하며 산업계에서 실질적으로 활용된 AI 사례.
1982: 물리학과 AI (홉필드 네트워크)
  • 존 홉필드(John Hopfield)
    통계 물리학의 원리를 응용한 연상 기억 모델 제안. 신경망이 에너지 함수를 최소화하는 방식으로 최적화될 수 있음을 수학적으로 증명(2024 노벨 물리학상의 배경).
1986: 연결주의의 부활
  • 제프리 힌튼(Geoffrey Hinton)
    Backpropagation(오차 역전파) 알고리즘 대중화. 다층 퍼셉트론(MLP) 학습이 가능해지며 신경망 연구 재점화.
1987: 두 번째 AI 겨울
  • Expert Systems의 붕괴
    비용 대비 효과 부족으로 시장 붕괴. 전용 하드웨어인 Lisp Machine의 몰락과 PC의 보급.
1988: 확률론과 통계의 부상
  • 주데아 펄 (Judea Pearl)
    베이지안 네트워크(Bayesian Network)를 통해 불확실성 하에서의 확률적 추론 체계 정립.
  • 통계적 기계 번역
    IBM 연구진이 규칙 기반 대신 대량의 데이터(의사록)를 통한 통계적 패턴 학습 방식을 기계 번역에 도입.
1989: CNN의 기초
  • 얀 르쿤(Yann LeCun)
    우편번호 판독을 위한 초기 합성곱 신경망 LeNet 개발. 현대 Computer Vision 및 Deep Learning 기술의 토대 마련.
1993: 특이점 (Singularity)
  • 버너 빈지 (Vernor Vinge)
    AI가 인간 지능을 초월하여 문명을 근본적으로 변화시키는 '기술적 특이점' 개념을 대중화.
1997: RNN의 혁신
  • LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)
    기존 순환 신경망(RNN)의 장기 의존성 문제를 해결한 모델. 음성 인식, 시계열 예측 등의 기반이 됨.
1997: Deep Blue의 승리
  • IBM Deep Blue
    세계 체스 챔피언 가리 카스파로프(Garry Kasparov)에게 승리. 연산 능력에 기반한 '기호주의 AI'의 상징적 성과.
1998: CNN 최적화
  • LeNet-5 최적화
    얀 르쿤과 요수아 벤지오가 역전파 알고리즘을 통해 CNN을 필기 인식에 효과적으로 적용하는 방법 정립.
2006: Deep Learning의 재정립
  • 제프리 힌튼(Geoffrey Hinton)
    심층 신뢰 신경망(DBN) 논문 발표. 기존 신경망의 한계를 극복하며 Deep Learning이라는 용어를 본격적으로 사용하기 시작.
2009: 데이터 중심 AI (ImageNet)
  • 페이페이 리(Fei-Fei Li)
    1,400만 장 이상의 라벨링된 이미지 데이터셋 ImageNet 공개. 데이터의 양과 질이 알고리즘 성능을 결정한다는 사실을 입증하며 딥러닝 혁명의 필수 기반 마련.
2010: DeepMind 창립
  • 데미스 하사비스(Demis Hassabis)
    런던에서 설립. '지능을 해결하여 모든 것을 해결한다(Solve Intelligence)'는 비전으로 AGI 연구 시작. 훗날 Google에 인수(2014)됨.
2011: IBM Watson의 퀴즈 제패
  • IBM Watson
    퀴즈 쇼 'Jeopardy!'에서 인간 챔피언들을 꺾고 우승. 자연어 처리(NLP) 기술을 통해 인간의 언어를 이해하고 답하는 AI의 가능성을 대중에게 각인.
2012: 비지도 학습의 가능성
  • Google Brain (Cat Experiment)
    앤드류 응과 제프 딘이 유튜브 영상에서 레이블 없이 고양이의 개념을 스스로 학습하는 거대 신경망 실험 성공.
2012: Deep Learning 혁명
  • AlexNet
    제프리 힌튼 연구팀이 이미지넷(ImageNet) 경진대회 우승. GPU를 활용한 압도적인 성능 차이로 현대적 AI 시대 개막.
2014: 생성형 AI의 씨앗 (GAN)
  • 이안 굿펠로우(Ian Goodfellow)
    '적대적 생성 신경망(GAN)' 발표. 생성자(Generator)와 판별자(Discriminator)의 경쟁 학습 방식을 도입하여 이미지 생성 기술의 비약적 발전 유도.
2015: OpenAI 설립
  • 배경
    구글의 딥마인드 인수에 따른 기술 독점을 견제하기 위해, 일론 머스크와 샘 알트만이 의기투합하여 시작됨.
  • 목표
    일리야 수츠케버(Ilya Sutskever) 등을 영입하여 '인류 모두에게 이익이 되는 안전한 AI'를 목표로 하는 비영리 연구소로 출범.
2016: 알파고 모멘트
  • AlphaGo
    Google DeepMind의 AlphaGo vs 이세돌 9단 대국. 강화학습(Reinforcement Learning)과 딥러닝의 결합이 보여준 충격.
2017: Transformer의 등장
  • Attention Is All You Need
    Google이 Transformer 아키텍처 제안. RNN을 대체하며 현대 거대언어모델(LLM)의 기술적 기원 확립.
2018: AI의 거장들 (Turing Award)
  • 수상자
    제프리 힌튼, 얀 르쿤, 요수아 벤지오(Yoshua Bengio) 공동 수상. Deep Learning 발전에 기여한 공로로 '컴퓨터 과학의 노벨상'인 튜링상 수상.
2020: LLM의 도약과 과학 AI
  • LLM (GPT-3)
    OpenAI가 1,750억 개 파라미터 모델을 공개. 별도의 튜닝 없이 소량의 예시만으로 과제를 수행하는 'Few-shot Learning' 능력 입증.
  • 과학 AI (AlphaFold 2)
    DeepMind가 '단백질 구조 예측 대회(CASP14)'에서 우승하며, 50년 난제였던 단백질 접힘 문제를 해결해 순수 과학 분야에서의 효용성 증명.
2021: Anthropic 설립
  • 다리오 아모데이(Dario Amodei)
    OpenAI의 상업화와 안전성 우려로 연구원들이 집단 퇴사하여 설립. 해석 가능하고 통제 가능한 AI(Claude) 개발 목표.
2022: 생성형 AI (GenAI) 대중화
  • ChatGPT
    대화형 인터페이스와 언어 모델을 통해 대중이 AI를 일상적으로 사용하는 계기 마련.
2023: xAI 설립
  • 일론 머스크(Elon Musk)
    '우주의 진정한 본질을 이해한다'는 목표로 설립. 기존 AI 모델들의 편향성을 비판하며 진실 추구(Truth-seeking) AI를 표방하는 Grok 출시.
2024: 멀티모달과 노벨상
  • 멀티모달 (Multimodal)
    GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 등 텍스트, 음성, 영상을 실시간 처리하는 모델 경쟁.
  • 노벨상 수상
    존 홉필드, 제프리 힌튼(노벨 물리학상) 및 데미스 하사비스(노벨 화학상) 수상으로 AI의 과학적 공로 인정.
2025: 생각하고(Reasoning), 행동하며(Agent), 몸을 가진(Physical) 지능 연구
  • 추론 모델 (Reasoning Models)
    Google Gemini, OpenAI o1/o3 등 '생각의 사슬(Chain of Thought)'을 통해 복잡한 문제를 해결하는 AI의 확산.
  • 에이전트 AI (Agentic AI)
    AI가 도구를 사용하고 스스로 계획을 세우는 '에이전트'의 상용화가 시작되었으나, 아직 부족함.
  • 물리적 지능(Physical AI)
    언어 모델을 넘어 로봇 공학(Humanoid)과 결합하려는 시도의 본격화.
  • 월드 모델 (World Models)
    물리 법칙과 공간의 인과관계를 시뮬레이션하려는 연구 심화.
  • 재귀적 자기 개선 (Recursive Self-Improvement)
